# The north star — what this project is ultimately for [USER]

The single most important orientation for anyone continuing this work. (Captured 2026-07-21 from the user directly.)

## The genie wish
An LLM that **LEARNS** and does complex **REASONING**, **much smaller than conventional models**, with an
ever-**EXPANDING**, **UPDATABLE** knowledge base.

## The sacred invariant (never traded away)
Realistically there will be compromises. When they come, **EXPANSION and GROWABILITY must NOT be lost.** Growth and
updatability are the point of the whole architecture; other properties may flex, these may not.

## The rest of the picture
- **Language is a personal BENCHMARK, not the endpoint** — the model being capable of language is how progress is judged, not the goal itself.
- **Multimodality (long-horizon):** the ability to plug in and add more "avenues" — modalities beyond text.
- **Observability dashboard (long-horizon):** stream and watch the thinking / processes as they happen.
- **From scratch, not a pretrained base:** chosen for **NOVELTY + full OWNERSHIP** — not because a pretrained base is technically impossible.
- **Compute:** rented H100, available **as long as necessary**. The scale path is feasible — but "much smaller than
  conventional" means the bet is **capability-per-parameter via the architecture**, not brute-force scale.

## Success priorities (most → least important) [USER]
1. **Conversation** (the top personal benchmark)
2. **Sentence generation** (the near-term threshold)
3. **A fully characterized architecture** (society / regimes / editability measured + understood)
4. **Shipping**

(An importance ranking, not a build sequence — (1) depends on (2) in practice.)

## How this colors the open decisions (`open-questions/`)
- **Growability being sacred makes the EXPERT-EVOLUTION scheme (Q0) central, not peripheral.** A birth/cull rule that
  lets a bad expert survive (the occupancy-fitness weakness) directly threatens healthy growth.
- **An updatable, expandable knowledge base leans toward MODULARITY (Q-regime)** — meaningful, addable/removable pieces —
  but that remains the user's call (redundancy buys robustness instead).
- **Autonomous wrong-detection (B, Q3) is not central to this north star** — consistent with the recommendation to cut B
  and focus effort on language + growth.

*These are neutral implications, not decisions — the four open questions are still the user's to answer.*
