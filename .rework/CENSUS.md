# The lever census — all 328 knobs, classified

P2 of the plan. Every knob in the old `_SPEC` gets exactly one verdict, an owning package, a unit and a
reason. A lever that vanishes without a decision is the outcome the owner specifically asked to prevent,
so completeness is checked mechanically against `_SPEC`: **328 declared, 328 covered, 0 missing.**

Produced by 12 agents, one per family, each working from the source rather than from recollection, and
audited by an adversarial reviewer. Every entry carries a reason; no drop has a reason shorter than 40
characters.

## Verdicts

| verdict | n | meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| `keep` | 100 | carries over unchanged in meaning |
| `rename` | 156 | carries over under its owner's generated name |
| `merge` | 25 | folded into another lever, which the reason names |
| `drop` | 41 | does not carry over |
| `promote-to-wire` | 6 | was never a lever: a value another package owns, arriving as a wire |

**41 dropped of 328.** The owner ruled that a mechanism never observed to fire is NOT thereby
proven useless -- several were inert because the *instrument* was broken. Dropping for "the report never
showed it firing" was forbidden; dropping for "nothing reads it", "duplicate off-switch" or "the
mechanism is going" was required to say so.
